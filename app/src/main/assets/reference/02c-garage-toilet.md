# Garage emergency toilet (indoor, separating)

For nights, storms, illness, or simply not wanting to cross the garden in the cold and rain. It works on the same principle as the garden toilet: keep urine and solids apart and cover the solids.

@image 4-garage-toilet.png | Separating box: urine to a sealed bottle, solids to a lidded bucket with cover material

## What you need
- A box or frame about 45 cm high, with a hinged top board and a toilet seat.
- A urine-diverting insert or front funnel, with a short hose into a 10 L screw-cap jerrycan.
- A 20 L lidded bucket for solids. Line it with compostable liners or a few sheets of newspaper.
- A lidded bin of sawdust or wood shavings with a scoop.
- A waterproof mat or tray under everything, and a privacy curtain if needed.
- A hand-wash station (water container with a tap, soap, towel), plus hand sanitiser.
- Gloves, a vinegar spray, spare liners, toilet roll in a lidded box, a torch.
- Optional: a vent duct from the box to the outside wall, with a 12 V computer fan (1-2 W) running from the AFERIY or a van battery. This keeps the garage completely odour-free.

## Where to put it
- Near the garage door or a window, for fresh air.
- On a level floor, away from fuel cans, gas bottles, chemicals and anything you are charging.
- Somewhere you can reach easily in the dark, with a torch hung in the same spot every time.

## Every use
1. Sit to use. Urine goes into the funnel.
2. Cover the solids with a scoop of sawdust.
3. Close the lid.
4. Wash your hands.

## Emptying
- Urine bottle: empty it daily or every other day into the soakaway trench, or pour it down the garden toilet's diverter so it reaches the tank. Rinse the bottle with a splash of vinegar.
- Solids bucket: when it is two-thirds full, put the lid on and carry it to the garden. Lift the ACTIVE pit's panel, tip the bucket in (liner and all if the liner is compostable), and cover with straw. Plastic bags go in the rubbish bin, never in the pit.
- Rinse the bucket with a little water and detergent and pour the rinse water into the pit. Put fresh cover material in the bottom before refitting it.

## Keeping the garage smell-free
- Keep the lid down at all times. With a vent fan, the lid-down box draws air away from you.
- Keep the urine out of contact with the solids. Fix any drip from the funnel straight away.
- Wipe the seat and funnel daily with vinegar spray.
- Never use bleach where it can meet urine.

## If the garden pits aren't ready yet
Use the garage toilet as your main toilet. Tie off each full bag of solids, label it, and store it in a lidded bin outdoors, away from water. Add the contents to pit A once it is built.
