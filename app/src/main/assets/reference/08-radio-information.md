# Radio and emergency information

! BBC Radio 4 long wave (198 kHz) was switched off permanently in June 2026. Do not plan around it.

## Where information will come from
- BBC local radio on FM: the main source of local emergency news. Find your station's FM frequency NOW, and write it here and on the radio.
- BBC Radio 4 and Radio 5 Live on FM and DAB.
- UK Emergency Alerts on mobile phones. These only work while the mobile network is up.
- Neighbours and community noticeboards: often the fastest local news when networks are down.

## Wind-up radio routine
- Wind or charge it weekly, even in calm times, and test it.
- Keep spare batteries if it takes them.
- Listen at set times - for example the morning and early-evening news. Constant listening drains the radio and adds stress.

## Useful numbers (written down, not just in the phone)
- Emergency: 999 or 112
- Power cut: 105
- NHS non-emergency: 111

## Meeting plan with a second person
- Agree a meeting point at home and a second one away from home.
- Agree a time to check in each day if you are apart and phones are down - for example, at the meeting point at noon.
- Leave a dated note inside the front door saying where you have gone, if you leave in the van.
