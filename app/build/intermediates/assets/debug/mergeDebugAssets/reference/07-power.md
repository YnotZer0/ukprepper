# Managing electricity off-grid

Your storage: the AFERIY P210 (2048 Wh), the van's LiFePO4 bank (2 x 50 Ah) and the garden bank (2 x 100 Ah), plus solar on the van and in the garden. The Energy & fuel screen turns this into days of supply for each month.

! UK winter solar is a fraction of summer output. Plan December on what is stored, not on what the panels might produce.

## Power priorities
1. Information and light: radio, phone, torches, LED lamps.
2. Water treatment and health: nothing here needs power - keep it that way.
3. Food cooling: the 12 V fridge is often the biggest load. In UK winter an insulated box in a cold shed may be enough, so turn the fridge off when outdoor temperatures allow.
4. Laptop: the ZBook running NOMAD/Ollama is a heavy load. Use it for short, planned sessions.

## Getting the most from solar
- Charge when the sun is out: run the laptop and top up power banks in the middle of the day.
- Keep panels clean and clear of shade. Even a small shadow cuts output a lot.
- Tilt the garden panels steeper in winter (about 60° from horizontal) and flatter in summer.

## Battery care
- LiFePO4 handles deep discharge well but must NOT be charged below 0 °C. Check that your charge controller and the AFERIY have low-temperature cut-offs, and keep the batteries somewhere that stays above freezing.
- If the garden batteries are lead-acid or AGM rather than LiFePO4, don't take them below 50 %, and set "Garden usable" to 0.5.
- Keep connections tight and dry. Fuse every positive lead close to the battery.

## Measure, don't guess
The AFERIY display shows watts in and out. Spend one day logging the fridge, the laptop under load, and phone charging. Enter the real figures in Energy & fuel.

## Phone battery tips
- Turn on battery saver.
- Lower the screen brightness. This app uses a dark theme to save power on your phone's OLED screen.
- Use airplane mode when there is no network. Searching for signal drains a battery fast.
