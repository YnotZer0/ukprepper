# Staying fit, alert and steady

Long disruptions wear people down through boredom, poor sleep and worry as much as through hardship. A routine is a survival tool.

## Daily structure
The Activities screen holds a default day. Adjust it, then stick to it:
- Get up and go to bed at the same times.
- Do the essential jobs (water, toilet, fire, food) at fixed times, so nothing is forgotten.
- Do one productive project a day: firewood, repairs, a skill.
- End the day by logging stock and planning tomorrow.

## Body
- Move for at least 30 minutes a day: a brisk walk, bodyweight squats, press-ups, step-ups, stretching.
- Save heavy work like sawing and splitting for daylight and the warmest part of the day, with breaks.
- Get outside in daylight every day. It protects sleep and mood.
- Look after your feet, teeth and skin: small problems grow when help is far away.

## Mind
- Learn a skill on a schedule: knots, first aid, foraging basics, repairs, a language.
- Read, write a short daily journal, play cards or board games.
- Limit news to set times.
- Notice the warning signs - poor sleep, irritability, low appetite, losing interest - and talk about them.

## Two people
- Agree who does which jobs, and swap the unpleasant ones fairly.
- Plan time apart as well as together.
- Hold a short daily check-in: what went well, what's worrying you, tomorrow's plan.

## Alcohol
Keep it minimal. It disrupts sleep, makes you colder, and impairs judgement when using axes, gas and fire.
